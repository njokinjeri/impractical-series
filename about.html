about the series

Impractical Series is a growing collection of generative and interactive experiments - built for the joy of building, not the need to ship.

why

To play with ideas that don't need a business case - canvas, shaders, physics, procedural geometry, and anything else that seems interesting at 2am.

how

TypeScript first. No frameworks unless the idea demands one. Each experiment ships as a standalone page with its own entry point. Built in public - all the wrong turns included.
