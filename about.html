about the series

A collection of experimental visual projects. This series tests the boundaries of standard web software, stepping away from traditional interfaces into a space driven entirely by pixels, logic, and play.

Philosophy

Every project starts as a visual question. Instead of building software to solve a business problem or optimize a commercial product, the goal here is to give spontaneous midnight ideas room to grow.

It is an intentional space to build simply and see what happens, documenting both the breakthroughs and the messy mistakes along the way.
