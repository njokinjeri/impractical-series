KYUBE

An interlocking basalt cube drifts through deep space. No atmosphere. No warmth. Yet something lives here.

Orbital Commands

Grab & Drag: Spin the cube to inspect every seam and crater.
Scroll / Pinch: Move in close to the glowing core, or pull back for the full debris field.
Xeno-Spore Switch: Strip the cube down to bare stone.
Sun / Core Mode: Switch between deep-space dark and full stellar light.
